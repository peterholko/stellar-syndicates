Peter (Nov 1 2026): the research panel should be "full screen like the
structure panel", with "larger graphics for research".

One emblem per programme, 121 in all, in the style of the six field emblems
(`client/art-src/research-topics-icons/research-topics-icons.png`). The emblem
appears on the research card, the detail panel, the queue and the active-research
bar. A programme whose emblem hasn't landed shows its field's emblem, so the
panel can ship before the art.

No new art for:
- the six field tabs: cut from the field sheet;
- what a programme unlocks: the building's own art, the station art
  (`stations/v2`) and the hull's model render (`ships/models-v1/previews`).

## Sheets for ChatGPT (Nov 3 2026)

Peter wanted the unique emblems "now". The 119 still owed are packaged as 21
ChatGPT sheets, six badges per message in the field sheet's own 3 × 2
arrangement: `research-emblem-sheets.md` (generated by
`client/scripts/build-research-sheet-prompts.mjs`). A landed sheet goes to
`client/art-src/research-emblems-v1/sheets/<name>.png`; `npm run assets:research`
slices it by its clear gaps against `sheets/sheets.json`. The single-emblem
prompts below stay the way to redo one badge: a `<key>.png` master wins over its
sheet cell.

## What to generate

- **121 images**, one per programme. `client/art-src/research-emblems-v1/prompts.json`
  holds every finished prompt and its reference images (the ore set's format:
  `mode`, `assets` with `key` and `prompt`). This doc's tables are the same
  content; `prompts.json` wins if they ever disagree.
- **Save as** `client/art-src/research-emblems-v1/<key>.png`. The file names are
  the programme ids.
- **Size:** 1254 × 1254, genuinely transparent (real alpha).
- **Image 1** for every emblem: the field sheet. 16 emblems also attach
  an **Image 2**, the game's own art of the ship or station they show (paths are in
  `prompts.json` and in the tables below; convert the `.webp` to PNG if the tool
  needs it).
- **Order:**
  1. Test three: `prop_drive_tuning`, `mat_deep_bores`, `weap_fire_control`. Lay them beside
     the field sheet. If the badge drifts (shape, rim, face colour), fix the shared
     prompt before the run.
  2. Batch 1: the other 40 Tier I–II emblems, making 43 including the tests.
     A new player sees these first.
  3. Batch 2: Tiers III–VIII, 78 emblems.

## Shared prompt

Replace `{name}`, `{field}`, `{motif}` and `{palette}` from the tables. Drop the
`{image2}` line unless the row names an Image 2.

```text
Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: one transparent research emblem for Stellar Syndicates, a premium dark sci-fi strategy game. It joins an existing emblem family: Image 1 is the approved sheet of six research field emblems, attached ONLY for the badge shape, rim, face, rendering, lighting and finish. Draw ONE new emblem. Do not copy any of the six motifs.
Emblem: {name}, a {field} research programme.
Motif: {motif}
{image2}Badge: exactly Image 1's badge: a hexagon with one point at the top, one at the bottom and flat vertical sides; a deep navy-black face, slightly lighter toward its centre; a thin dark charcoal rim with a faint bevelled highlight along its upper-left edges. The motif sits inside the rim, centred, about 70% of the face's width. Nothing crosses the rim.
Colour: {palette}. Everything else stays in the family's navy, charcoal and silver.
Framing: square 1:1 canvas, 1254 x 1254 pixels. The hexagon is centred: its top and bottom points about 6% from the canvas edges (88% of the canvas height), its flat sides about 80% of the canvas width apart.
Style: Image 1's crisp, painterly game-emblem rendering: bold simple shapes, clean edges, soft light from the upper left, glow only where the colour line asks for it. One clear motif that still reads at 48 px: a few large forms, restrained small detail.
Background: genuinely transparent PNG with real alpha outside the hexagon. No drop shadow, outer glow, grey backdrop or vignette around the badge.
Constraints: exactly one badge. No text, letters, numbers, Roman numerals, labels, logos, watermark, second badge, frame or UI.
```

`{image2}`, when the row has one:

```text
Image 2 is {what the row's Image 2 is}. Use it ONLY for that design's silhouette and details: draw it once, in an elevated three-quarter view, as the motif, in Image 1's emblem rendering. Ignore its own lighting and background.
```

  dark rim, faint upper-left bevel.
- 1254 × 1254 with real alpha outside the hexagon: no drop shadow, outer glow
