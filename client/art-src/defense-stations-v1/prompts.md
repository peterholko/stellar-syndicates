# Defense stations · art prompts (v1)

The five-level orbital defense ladder: Defense Platform, Orbital Battery,
Defense Station, Citadel, Star Fortress. Run them in this order:

1. **Lineup** fixes the family look and the growth from level to level.
2. **Five-view reference sheets**, one per level, with the approved lineup crop
   as Image 1, for 3D reconstruction like the hull reference sheets.
3. **Icons**, one per level, for the system panel ladder.

Shared design language: every level grows around the same hexagonal armored
core with a round cyan reactor eye (the System View glyph is already a
hexagon with an eye). Each level keeps the previous level visible at its heart.
Turret counts are exact: 1, 2, 3, 4, then 6 plus the Sunlance. The material
family is the player fleet's: pale titanium, charcoal gunmetal, restrained
brass, small cyan lights.

## 1 · Lineup

```
Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: design lineup sheet for a five-level orbital defense station progression in the dark sci-fi strategy game Stellar Syndicates.
Show FIVE stations in one row on a plain white 3:1 landscape sheet, left to right, each the next upgrade of the same structure. All five use the SAME camera: an elevated three-quarter top-down view (about 40 degrees pitch), light from the upper left, and ONE consistent scale, so the growth is obvious: widths roughly 1 : 1.4 : 2.2 : 3 : 6. The fifth station dominates the right side of the sheet. Every station is fully visible with clear space between them.
SHARED DESIGN: every level is built around the same hexagonal armored core with a round cyan-lit reactor eye on top. Each upgrade keeps the previous level visible at its heart and adds structure around it, like a fortress adding rings of walls. Weapon hardpoints are heavy turrets on raised armored mounts.
1. DEFENSE PLATFORM: a compact, low hexagonal gun platform. Exactly ONE heavy twin-barrel turret just forward of the eye, six short radiator fins at the corners, two tiny point-defense nodes.
2. ORBITAL BATTERY: the same core inside a broader chamfered hexagonal armored collar. Exactly TWO turrets on raised barbettes at the two front corners: one a long single-barrel beam emitter with cyan focusing rings, the other a stubby heavy twin mass driver. Small point-defense nodes on the other corners.
3. DEFENSE STATION: the battery as a hub with exactly THREE armored arms at 120 degrees, each ending in a heavy twin-barrel turret (three turrets in total). Between the two rear arms, one open drone hangar with small parked drone craft on launch rails. An empty docking cradle along one arm, sized for a destroyer.
4. CITADEL: the station enclosed by a thick hexagonal armored outer wall with angled buttresses, like a star fort. Exactly FOUR heavy turret bastions on the front and side corners of the wall. Two utility bays on the rear corners: a domed shield generator with brass emitter pylons, and a minelayer with a rack of launch tubes. A tall central command tower; overlapping armored blast-door petals frame the core.
5. STAR FORTRESS: a colossal ring fortress, the capstone. A massive armored outer ring joined by six thick spokes to the citadel at its hub. Exactly SIX heavy triple-barrel turret batteries spaced around the ring. Three utility bays on the ring: a large drone hangar, a shield-generator dome, and a cluster of three tall interdiction-field spires. One enormous spinal SUNLANCE cannon crosses the whole diameter through the hub: a long segmented lance with focusing rings and heat-sink fins. On the far half of the ring, broad overlapping gold heat-shield vanes and solar collector petals (the side that faces its star). It must look far vaster and heavier than any warship.
STYLE: precise colored hard-surface 3D game concept rendering, clean contours, fine panel lines, pale silver titanium armor plates, charcoal gunmetal structure, restrained brass hardware, small cyan lights, mild wear, soft neutral illumination, no harsh shadows. Each silhouette must read clearly from above at small size.
No starfield, no star, no planets, no other ships, no weapons fire, no beams, no glow halos, no scale bars, no logos, no watermark.
Text exactly once each, small and charcoal, centered under its station: "DEFENSE PLATFORM", "ORBITAL BATTERY", "DEFENSE STATION", "CITADEL", "STAR FORTRESS".
```

## 2 · Five-view reference sheet (one per level)

Attach the approved lineup crop of that level as Image 1 and a hull reference
sheet (for example `battleship-reference-v1/battleship-reference.png`) as
Image 2. Replace `{NAME}` and `{DESIGN}` with the level's entry below.

```
Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: fully textured five-view 2D orthographic orbital station reference sheet for accurate 3D reconstruction in the Stellar Syndicates fleet.
Image 1 is the authoritative design of this station. Expand it to exactly FIVE physically consistent views of that SAME station. Image 2 is only the fleet's reference-sheet presentation and material style; do not copy its ship silhouette.
DESIGN:
{NAME}: {DESIGN}
FRONT is the station's weapon face: the side its main turrets (and on the Star Fortress, the Sunlance muzzle) point toward.
LAYOUT: wide 2:1 landscape white sheet, high native detail. TOP and BOTTOM as two large plan drawings on the left 58% of the canvas, FRONT facing UP in both, identical outline and station positions. Right 42%: FRONT elevation at the top, BACK in the middle, SIDE at the bottom with FRONT to the LEFT. Clean charcoal title at the upper left and a small uppercase label under each of the five drawings. Generous separation, no sixth drawing, every view complete inside the sheet.
PROJECTION: true orthographic, parallel projection, ZERO camera pitch on FRONT, BACK and SIDE; TOP and BOTTOM have no perspective. Keep turret, bay and spire counts and positions identical across every view; far-side items overlap naturally in the elevations. Do not add unexplained guns, bays or antennas.
TOP is the in-game view: a bold outer silhouette and clearly visible turrets that still read at 64 px.
BOTTOM: fully detailed ventral armor with seams, hatches, radiator grilles and docking clamps, same outline and openings as TOP; no turrets printed on it. True holes stay blank white. All elevations carry coherent armor texture, not empty gray blocks.
STYLE: precise colored hard-surface 2D technical concept illustration, clean contours, fine panel lines, pale silver titanium plates, charcoal gunmetal structure, muted brass hardware, small cyan lights. Mild wear, soft neutral illumination, no strong highlights or cast shadows. Match the fleet reference style. No scenery, checkerboard, grid, dimensions, logos, weapons fire, glow or watermark.
Text exactly once each: "{NAME}", "TOP", "BOTTOM", "FRONT", "BACK", "SIDE".
```
