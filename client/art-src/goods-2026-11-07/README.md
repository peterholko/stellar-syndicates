# Shipbuilding goods · 7 November 2026

Fourteen inventory masters generated with the built-in `image_gen` tool from
[`docs/shipbuilding-art-prompts.md`](../../../docs/shipbuilding-art-prompts.md).
All masters are native 1254 × 1254 RGBA PNGs with real transparency. The selected
generated bytes are retained unchanged; the source bounds occupy 82–89% of the
square's longest dimension.

The ore preview and Conductive Metals master supplied the house style. The four
warp cores share the Fission Core body and camera, with amber-orange, blue-white,
gold and violet emitter variants. The two reactors share the wider toroidal
vessel. The Beam Battery uses the Pulse Laser's materials and camera.

[`prompts.json`](prompts.json) records each exact generation prompt, reference
paths and follow-up framing prompts. [`generation-manifest.json`](generation-manifest.json)
records source and reference hashes, generation history, dimensions, alpha and
framing measurements. Framing corrections were generated edits; no master was
cropped, resized or had its alpha altered afterward.

Run `npm run assets:derive` in `client/` to trim and centre the masters at the
shared 90% fill in `public/art/ui_icons/resource/goods-v1/<slug>-{64,128}.png`.
This folder is last in the goods source list, so its `fusion_core.png` supersedes
the older plant-heart image. Fusion Reactor now uses `fusion_reactor`; Weapon
Mounts keeps the commodity slug `armaments`. All fourteen entries use their new
goods art in both the icon registry and commodity rows, and their temporary SVGs
have been removed.

## Validation

- The derivative build completed with 32 goods and 28 new derivatives.
- All fourteen masters match their selected generated source hashes; dimensions,
  transparency, margins and centring were measured before delivery.
- A browser review at 32, 64 and 128 px checked the family alignment, distinct
  silhouettes and materials beside Conductive Metals and Titanium.
- The ore economy, equipment, shipyard view and standalone industry tests passed,
  as did TypeScript and the edited files' whitespace check.
- The combined `test:industry` command reaches an unrelated existing failure in
  `test-industrial-expansion.mjs:276`: its fleet mock omits `CORE_ENERGY` and the
  core grade now used by the route estimate. This art delivery does not alter
  that fixture or the route logic.

## Warp drives · 8 November 2026

The four drive placeholders have been replaced with native 1254 × 1254 RGBA
masters generated with the built-in `image_gen` tool. Zephyr was generated first;
each of the other three is a direct edit of that exact Zephyr image. Their
camera, steel body, copper conduit bands and lighting form one matched family.

| Master | Emitter and variant detail | Measured fill |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [`zephyr_drive.png`](zephyr_drive.png) | Amber-orange; plain bell | 85.7% |
| [`gale_drive.png`](gale_drive.png) | Blue-white; thin inner ring and slightly longer bell | 87.6% |
| [`tempest_drive.png`](tempest_drive.png) | Gold; three short gold vanes and brighter copper bands | 85.8% |
| [`maelstrom_drive.png`](maelstrom_drive.png) | Violet; faint internal spiral and black throat collar | 85.7% |

Exact prompts and Zephyr reference paths are appended to `prompts.json`.
The generation manifest records the selected source hashes, replaced placeholder
hashes, dimensions, alpha and bounds. All four saved masters match their original
generated bytes, with clear corners and centred silhouettes. No crop, resize or
alpha alteration was applied to these masters.

The drive icon wiring was already present; the icons were derived on Nov 8 2026.
