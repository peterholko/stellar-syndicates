# Travian: Legends: raid income vs own production (evidence notes)

Labels used below: **[Legends]** = Travian: Legends rules or sources (2019–2026). **[T4]** = Travian 4 era before Legends (about 2012–2018). **[T3]** = 2006–2011. **[Kingdoms]** = Travian Kingdoms, a separate game from the same company, included only for contrast. **[Anecdote]** = one player's account. **[Excerpt-only]** = text seen only in a search-engine excerpt because the page itself could not be loaded (Travian Fandom returned HTTP 402, Reddit and its mirrors returned 403, the official forum hosts wbb.forum.travian.com and archive.forum.travian.com did not resolve, and travian.com news pages rendered empty to the fetch tool).

## 1. Weekly statistics: Top-10 Raiders of the week vs typical production

### Takeaway
Legends has an in-game weekly Top-10 Raiders board (medals every Monday 00:00 server time), but no Legends-era (2019–2026) weekly values are publicly archived anywhere I could reach. The best concrete weekly figures are T4-era anecdotes. One player was the #5 raider in week 1 with 500,245 resources and #3 in week 2 with 805,308 (uk2, 2013/14, Gaul, speed not stated). That is about 3,000–4,800 resources/hour, roughly 10–16× the production of a ~100-pop early village. This matches the wiki claim that top Teutons earn 10–20× their field income in a speed server's first month.

### Cited Findings
- [Legends] Weekly player Top-10 categories are Attackers, Defenders, Raiders and PvE. PvE is defined as "kills vs. Natars and unoccupied oases". Medals are granted "every Monday at 00:00 (server time) for the previous week's performance". Alliance categories are Attack, Defense, Raiding and Climbers. Streak medals are awarded for Top-3 in the same category 3/5/10 times. The article does **not** define what "Raiders" measures. — [How Medals work (Travian support)](https://support.travian.com/en/articles/168-how-medals-work)
- [Legends] The weekly Top-10 attacker ranking was split into a PvE ranking (attacks on unoccupied oases and Natars) and a PvP ranking (everything else). No date is given. — [Unofficial Travian: "Travian: Legends ~ 6 years of changes at a glance" (orig. May 2023, republished 2025)](https://unofficialtravian.com/2025/01/travian-legends-6-years-of-changes-at-a-glance/)
- [Legends] A community medals guide (Jan 2025, updated Feb 2025) lists "Top-10 Raiders" without defining what counts toward it and gives no example values. — [Unofficial Travian: Game Secrets ~ Medals](https://unofficialtravian.com/2025/01/game-secrets-medals/)
- [Legends] The game tracks each player's "average hourly raid income over the past 7 days". The pushing-protection system adds it to the sender's hourly production when computing sending limits. — [Automatic resource-sharing limits (Travian support)](https://support.travian.com/en/articles/125-automatic-resource-sharing-limits)
- [T4, Anecdote] "T4 Start Game Guide" by Chris (Lord Aslan), posted Jan 7, 2016 and describing his uk2 2013/14 game as a Gaul (server speed not stated):
  - First 72 hours: 31 Phalanx and 29 TT.
  - Week 1: "300 tt and ended the week rank 5 raider, having raided 500245 res".
  - Week 2: settled a 150% 15-cropper on day 8, then "ended week two rank 3 raider, having raided 805308".
  - Method: sends raids "once an hour"; farm-list setup took "around 3 hours work".
  - Stopping point: "I usually don't bother raiding past week 5 or so."
  - Raid sizing table (village production per hour by population): 2 pop = 80, 70 pop = 180, 100+ pop = 300+.
  — [T4 Start Game Guide (lordsoftravian forum)](https://lordsoftravian.forumotion.com/t5-t4-start-game-guide)
- [T3, 2009 guide, Excerpt-only] "What makes the difference from a top 10 raider to a top 100 raider is the amount of times you send your troops to raid. Normally you hit top 10 Raider after you kept 50 or more raids at all moments going for the last 48 hours straight." — [Travian Library: Growth Guide (2009)](https://travianlibrary.wordpress.com/2009/03/13/growth-guide/)
- [T4, undated guide] A Teuton guide tells players to aim for 1st–3rd on the raiding leaderboards and top 2 on the weekly boards. Its army milestones are 150–250 clubs by day 8, 300 clubs plus ~300 barbarians by day 20, and 500 clubs later. — [T4 Teuton Full Guide (blogspot)](http://travian4guides.blogspot.com/p/t4-teuton-full-guide.html)
- [Version not stated, Excerpt-only] "It is not atypical a week into the server to see Teutons with 300+ outgoing attacks." — [Travian Wiki (Fandom): Farm](https://travian.fandom.com/wiki/Farm)
- [Legends] Official benchmark for a developed village at x2 speed: full economic development (resource fields to level 10, resource buildings to level 5) costs about 1,350,000 resources and yields 16,800 resources/hour. This comes from an official Travian blog post by Ameno (Apr 27, 2023), reposted in 2025. — [Unofficial Travian repost: "Early development: Return on Investment"](https://unofficialtravian.com/2025/01/early-development-return-on-investment/)
- [Kingdoms] Kingdoms has a "Top 10 Robbers of the Week" ranking, and Robber Camp rewards (PvE) count toward it. This is a different game from Legends. — [Kingdoms support: Robber camps](https://support.kingdoms.com/en/articles/21-robber-camps)

### Inferences
- The T4 week-1 figure (500,245/week) works out to about 71,000/day or 2,980/hour. Week 2 (805,308/week) is about 115,000/day or 4,790/hour. A week-1 or week-2 account usually has well under 300 pop, and the same guide puts a 100+ pop village at about 300+/hour. So a top-5 raider was taking in roughly **10–16× the production of a ~100-pop village**. Caveats: speed not stated, and the author probably had 1–2 villages.
- One fully developed x2 village (16,800/hour, or about 8,400/hour at x1 if output scales linearly with speed) produces more than week-2 top-3 raid income (~4,800/hour). The raid multiple therefore falls fast once an account owns a few developed villages.
- "Top 10 Raiders" is mainly a measure of **send volume and attention** (raids in flight around the clock), not raw army size. That fits the 2009 guide and the hourly-send routine in the T4 guide.

### Gaps
- I found no public record of actual Legends (2019–2026) Top-10 Raiders numbers. The weekly lists live in the in-game Statistics screen, and the sources most likely to hold screenshots could not be read (Reddit/mirrors 403, Fandom 402, official forum hosts not resolving).
- It is undocumented whether Legends' "Raiders" counts only loot from player villages or also oasis and Natar loot. The oasis animal bounty goes to the hero inventory, so it may not count at all.
- The uk2 server speed is not stated, so the T4 multiples carry a speed uncertainty.

## 2. Expert guides and community consensus: how many times own production, by stage; when raiding stops paying

### Takeaway
The sources agree on a declining multiple:
- **Early game:** raiding is the main income for good raiders, especially Teutons. The wiki claim is 10–20× field income in a speed server's first month.
- **Late game:** it becomes a supplement. At about 30 villages, production is over 500k/hour and top raiders add 300k+/hour (≤0.6× production, roughly a third of total income).
- **Stopping point:** several guide authors stop farming players around week 4–5 and put raids into fields.

Official Legends guidance sets a break-even test: troops must pay back their own training and upkeep. For oasis hunting, troop losses should be at most half the bounty.

### Cited Findings
- [Version not stated, likely T4, Excerpt-only] Travian Wiki "Farm" page:
  - "Farming is the primary source of income for most good players in the early game, particularly Teuton."
  - "in a speed server's first month, top Teutons can earn ten to twenty times their field income".
  - "Once a player reaches around thirty villages, their income is likely well over half a million per hour, but the top raiders will supplement this with 300,000+ from farming."
  - "farming becomes a less important component of a player's income, even though more resources are available to raid" (as fields grow and players run several villages).
  - A late-game raider's field income dwarfs small raids, so late raiders pick farms they can hit with far more troops.
  — [Travian Wiki (Fandom): Farm](https://travian.fandom.com/wiki/Farm)
- [Legends, official blog, x2 speed] Ameno, "Early development: Return on Investment" (official Travian blog, Apr 27, 2023):
  - A full village economy pays back in about 80 hours (1.35M cost, 16,800/hour).
  - Hero's Mansion 15→20 for a third oasis costs 1,595,070 for +2,100 crop/hour, about 607 hours payback.
  - "The army should either give additional income that covers costs to their training and upkeep" (or be trained only once the economy can absorb the cost).
  - Farming accounts should use only farm income for extra units at first, split about 60% army / 40% economy.
  - Non-farming accounts should train troops only after at least "2 fully developed" villages.
  — [Unofficial Travian repost](https://unofficialtravian.com/2025/01/early-development-return-on-investment/)
- [Legends, official support] Oasis farming:
  - Simulate first, and send only if the "resource cost of your troop losses is at least twice lower than the resources your hero gains from the oasis".
  - Hero farming becomes efficient at hero level 30+.
  - Avoid distant oases early; "mid-game, experienced farmers often cover the whole map".
  — [Oasis Farming (After Beginner Protection)](https://support.travian.com/en/articles/185-oasis-farming-after-beginner-protection)
- [T4, Anecdote] The uk2 2013/14 Gaul (#5 raider week 1, #3 week 2):
  - Stopped at about week 5: "I usually don't bother raiding past week 5 or so".
  - Moved weeks 4–5 raids into fields.
  - Argues early parties are a poor investment compared with fields.
  — [T4 Start Game Guide](https://lordsoftravian.forumotion.com/t5-t4-start-game-guide)
- [T4, undated] Teuton guide:
  - Raiding is the core strategy: "just keep raiding larger numbers of villages and farms".
  - Raided resources fund expansion ("most of your resources will go to the second village").
  - Recommends two sitters to keep raids running 24/7.
  — [T4 Teuton Full Guide](http://travian4guides.blogspot.com/p/t4-teuton-full-guide.html)
- [Legends-era community, Excerpt-only] A r/travian post from about Oct 2026 calls raiding the best source of income, giving "a much higher ceiling on what is possible with an account" than "simming" (pure building). — [r/travian "noob advice" (mirror; page returned 403)](https://r.datuan.dev/r/travian/comments/1uhrwxl/noob_advice/)
- [T3, Anecdote, 2008] An anonymous comment claims "(24/7 raiding) village growth is 5 times that of someone who checks in twice a day for a minute or 2". No data given. — [JayIsGames review comments](https://jayisgames.com/review/travian.php)
- [Legends, low-quality simulation, Jan 2025] A blog models 100 Legionnaires (40 attack, 50 carry, 6 fields/hour) sent in pairs to 50 farms for 15 hours/day.
  - Result: 1,226 raids × 50 average = 61,300/day (about 2,550/hour).
  - It claims the 100 Legionnaires (40,000 resources) pay back in "less than one day".
  - The model is internally inconsistent (it calls raids 100% successful, then hedges "even if only a portion of these raids are successful"), and its yields are assumed, not observed.
  — [MMORTS Guides: Beginner's Guide to Raiding in Travian Legends](https://mmortsguides.wordpress.com/2025/01/05/beginners-guide-to-raiding-in-travian-legends/)
- [Kingdoms, Excerpt-only] Kingdoms micro-farming guide:
  - Many small raids add up, but regular losses "can quickly consume the resources you're gaining".
  - Size troops to the reports.
  - Farming an active player too much "may awaken a sleeping giant".
  — [Kingdoms: The Art of Micro-Farming](https://support.kingdoms.com/en/articles/155-the-art-of-micro-farming)

### Inferences
- The wiki's 30-village figure ("well over half a million per hour") matches 30 × 16,800/hour = 504,000/hour, the official x2 full-village rate. Its late-game numbers therefore likely describe a ~x2–x3 server.
- On that basis the community rule of thumb runs from about **10–20× production (month 1)** to about **≤0.6× production** (top raiders at 30 villages, i.e. ≤~37% of total income). That is a 20–30-fold fall in the raid multiple over a server's life.
- In the T4 guides, farming players stops paying around **weeks 4–5**. By then inactive farms have been deleted or emptied, neighbours have crannies and troops, and the same resources invested in fields compound. Dedicated hammer/raider accounts keep farming as a supplement into the late game (wiki).
- The official ROI framing (2023) is effectively the designers' own break-even rule for raiding armies: loot must cover training plus upkeep.

### Gaps
- No Legends-era quantitative survey of raid income by week exists in what I could reach. The "10–20×" and "300k+/hour" figures come from a wiki page of unknown date and version, read only through search excerpts.
- No mid-game numbers (weeks 3–10) were found for any version.
- No tribe-specific numbers (Huns, Egyptians, Spartans, Vikings) for raid yield as a multiple of production were found.

## 3. Where raid income comes from, and how the supply changes over a server's life

### Takeaway
Inactive ("grey") accounts are the canonical farm. Guides say never to farm known actives. Since about 2021–2023 Legends has added a large, officially promoted **PvE** source: the hero gets a resource bounty for every oasis animal killed, even if all attackers die. Natars also count as PvE. Robber hideouts and camps exist only in Kingdoms. Player-farm supply is front-loaded: abandoned starts get deleted, and guides quit around week 5. Oasis animals respawn and keep yielding, scaled by army and hero strength.

### Cited Findings
- [T4, Anecdote] Target rules from the uk2 guide:
  - "Under no circumstances should we put players we know are active, in our farm lists." Raiding actives is "a waste of time when you can be raiding an inactive player".
  - Scout villages over ~25 pop for animals, and everyone over 40 pop in week 1.
  - Inactive farms are auto-deleted after "approx 7 days" under 2 pop.
  — [T4 Start Game Guide](https://lordsoftravian.forumotion.com/t5-t4-start-game-guide)
- [Version not stated, Excerpt-only] The best farms are often larger players who have fallen inactive, and they have "a high turnover rate as they delete". — [Travian Wiki (Fandom): Farm](https://travian.fandom.com/wiki/Farm)
- [T3, 2008 comment] "New accounts that are created then abandoned by inactive / curious players continue to produce resources for 14 days"; "These 'farms' are perpetually raided for resources". — [JayIsGames review comments](https://jayisgames.com/review/travian.php)
- [Kingdoms, Excerpt-only] Inactive or less active villages are common targets, and scouting is often unnecessary for grey villages. Villages are automatically removed from farm lists when their activity status changes. — [Kingdoms: The Art of Micro-Farming](https://support.kingdoms.com/en/articles/155-the-art-of-micro-farming)
- [Legends, official] Oasis farming is called "one of the most impactful changes in Travian: Legends".
  - Killing a nature unit adds a bounty to the hero's inventory. The hero need not fight, and the full bounty is kept even if all attackers die.
  - Bounty per resource type: rats 40, wild boars 80, bears 120, crocodiles and tigers 120, elephants 200.
  - Initial protection, during which no animals respawn: 5 days at x1, 3 days at x2/x3, 2 days at x5, 1 day at x10. After it ends, animals respawn quickly, starting weak.
  — [Early Game Oasis Farming (Travian support)](https://support.travian.com/en/articles/190-early-game-oasis-farming)
- [Legends] Community description, posted May 4, 2023, says the feature arrived "within the last 2 years":
  - Oases start with weak animals (e.g., 3 bats, 2 spiders).
  - Spiders, snakes and bats are also 40 per resource; wolves 80.
  - Oasis production starts only after the original animals are removed.
  - The author calls it a change that "affected the gameplay a lot".
  — [Unofficial Travian: Early game oasis farming](https://unofficialtravian.com/2025/01/early-game-oasis-farming-feature-description/). The same site says it changed second-village strategies. — [6 years of changes](https://unofficialtravian.com/2025/01/travian-legends-6-years-of-changes-at-a-glance/)
- [Legends, official] Oasis economics at 1x:
  - Unoccupied production per hour: e.g., Lumber Type 1 = 40/10/10/11; Crop Type 2 = 10/10/10/81; Type 3 (Natarian area) = 80/10/10/11.
  - Storage: "1,000 each" (Type 1) or "2,000 each" (Types 2/3).
  - Production starts only after Beginner Protection ends.
  - A raid steals up to 10% of stored resources, which "regenerate fully over 10 minutes" (a raid 7 minutes later takes 7%).
  — [Oasis (Travian support)](https://support.travian.com/en/articles/48-oasis). The wiki says the 10%-per-10-minutes rule applies to *player-owned* oases (max 10% of the owning village's resources per 10 minutes). — [Travian Wiki (Fandom): Farm, Excerpt-only](https://travian.fandom.com/wiki/Farm)
- [Legends, official] Oasis troop needs:
  - Iron/Clay oases: fast cavalry in pairs (Teutons: 1 Paladin + 1 Teutonic Knight).
  - Wood/Crop oases: about 100 units per target for large armies.
  - Hun farmers use 6/6/6 or 6/6/3 setups.
  - Requirements vary by map: some areas need 2+2 cavalry, others 20–30 units (iron/clay) or 50–100 (wood/crop).
  - Annual Special mountain regions are rich in iron oases (Huns recommended).
  — [Oasis Farming (After Beginner Protection)](https://support.travian.com/en/articles/185-oasis-farming-after-beginner-protection)
- [Legends] Players in beginner's protection can still attack or raid Natarian villages and unoccupied oases. — [Unofficial Travian: Beginner's Protection (Oct 2025)](https://unofficialtravian.com/2025/10/beginners-protection/)
- [Legends, 2024 Annual Special / 2025 spring round, Excerpt-only] In Northern Legends, raid ships are the only ships that can be added to a farm list, and Viking raid ships are "the fastest in the world". — [Travian: Northern Legends (travian.com news)](https://www.travian.com/international/news/2025/03/20/travian-northern-legends-spring-round/); [Unofficial Travian: Northern Legends mechanics](https://unofficialtravian.com/2025/10/northern-legends-game-mechanics/)
- [Legacy forum, Excerpt-only] Top raiders in a server's first weeks usually aren't raiding oases yet; advice was to raid everything in a wide radius and put most resources into troops. Pre-Legends; host no longer resolves. — [Travian forum archive: "Top 10 raiders question"](http://archive.forum.travian.com/showthread.php?t=145239)
- [Kingdoms only] Robber hideouts are personal and contain resources and stolen goods. Robber camps are shared. — [Kingdoms: Robber hideouts](https://support.kingdoms.com/en/articles/15-robber-hideouts). The May 2025 rework moved camps into the wilderness, removed their attacks, added a rally system, and gives diminishing returns when too many players pile onto one camp (Excerpt-only). — [Kingdoms blog: "They've Found Us! A Robber's Take on Being Reworked"](https://blog.kingdoms.com/theyve-found-us-a-robbers-take-on-being-reworked/)

### Inferences
- An unoccupied oasis refills slowly: about 71–111 resources/hour at x1, so about 1,700–2,700/day, capped at 4,000–8,000 stock. That is small next to a developed village (~8,400/hour at x1, implied by the official 16,800/hour at x2). The real PvE prize in Legends is the **animal bounty** (160–800 resources per animal killed, all four types combined).
- Example: the T4-era starting oasis in the uk2 guide (14 rats, 8 spiders, 6 boars) would be worth about 5,440 resources in bounty under current Legends values. That is hypothetical, because the bounty did not exist then.
- Player-farm supply peaks early. Abandoned starts, beginners just out of protection and early quitters are plentiful in weeks 1–3, then thin out through deletion, cranny building and alliance protection. That is why guides stop around week 5. PvE (oases, Natars) does not deplete with player churn, and Legends now officially promotes it.

### Gaps
- No quantitative split of raid income by source (inactive players vs active players vs oases vs Natars) exists for any Legends server in what I found.
- Not verified whether Legends still deletes abandoned accounts after ~7 days at under 2 pop (a T4-era claim).
- No figures found for Natar raid yields in Legends.

## 4. The victim's side: losses, protection, and new-player retention

### Takeaway
A small Legends player relies on:
- non-cancellable beginner's protection (5 days at x1, extendable once by 3);
- a cranny (level 10 hides 2,000 per resource; Gauls 3,000);
- spending or queueing resources, and keeping resources in the hero inventory or in transit;
- alliances.

Anything above cranny capacity in warehouse, granary or open market offers is lootable. Critics and players have long called the post-protection period "Early Game Hell" and a churn driver. The only explicit loot cap Travian Games has shipped is on selected **Kingdoms** special worlds, not Legends.

### Cited Findings
- [Legends, community, Oct 2025] Beginner's protection:
  - Length: x1 = 5 days (+3 on request), x2 and x3 = 3 days (+3), x5 = 2 days (+2), x10 = 1 day (+1).
  - It can be extended once, "It is not possible to end the protection earlier", and once ended it cannot be regained.
  - Protected players cannot send troops or resources to others, and "No troops can be sent to a player who is still under beginner's protection".
  — [Unofficial Travian: Beginner's Protection](https://unofficialtravian.com/2025/10/beginners-protection/)
- [T4.4+, Excerpt-only] "Since Travian 4.4 it is impossible to raid players in the first 5 days due to beginner's protection, which you can no longer cancel. Unlike what old guides say, you should build resource fields but make sure to start training clubswingers before your beginner's protection ends." — [Travian Wiki (Fandom): Teutons](https://travian.fandom.com/wiki/Teutons)
- [Older rules, Excerpt-only] An older wiki page says each server's protection "starts out at 3 days, with the exception of speed which starts at 1 day". That conflicts with the current 5 days at x1. It advises a level-10 cranny before protection ends and calls the game's new-player placement "not a for sure thing". — [Travian Wiki (Fandom): Guide to not be farmed (part)](https://travian.fandom.com/wiki/Guide_to_not_be_farmed_(part))
- [Legends] Raiding rules:
  - Cranny: level 1 protects 200 of each resource, level 10 protects 2,000; Gauls protect 3,000 at level 10.
  - Lootable: warehouses, granaries and resources offered on the marketplace.
  - Resources in the hero inventory cannot be taken during an attack.
  - Raids don't reduce loyalty, and rams and catapults don't act.
  - Teuton heroes give a 20% plunder bonus, which a bag can raise.
  — [Unofficial Travian: Battle Mechanics (Oct 2025)](https://unofficialtravian.com/2025/10/battle-mechanics/). The same cranny figures (200 → 2,000; Gaul 3,000) appear in Travian's developer support "Battle Mechanics" article, seen only as a search excerpt (it returned 404 on fetch). — [dev-support.traviangames.com](https://dev-support.traviangames.com/support/solutions/articles/202000089877-battle-mechanics)
- [Conflict] Cranny modifiers differ by source:
  - The wiki says Teutons as a tribe "can steal anything from the top 20%* of the total cranny space for each resource" (older, Excerpt-only). — [Travian Wiki (Fandom): Teutons](https://travian.fandom.com/wiki/Teutons)
  - Kingdoms ties a 20% "cranny dip" to the Teuton *hero*, applying only to units travelling with him. — [Kingdoms: Your hero](https://support.kingdoms.com/en/articles/44-your-hero)
  - Older sources give Gauls a doubled cranny (4,000), not 1.5×.
- [Legends, official] Defensive advice:
  - The cranny is one of the most cost-effective early-game defences.
  - Keep storage low when attacks are expected: spend on buildings or troops, or send resources to allies. Use the building queue and Master Builder while away.
  - Resources in transit cannot be stolen; only stored resources and open trade offers are at risk.
  - Raided resources go to the attacker's warehouse and granary.
  — [Guide: Resources Explained (Travian support)](https://support.travian.com/en/articles/214-guide-resources-explained)
- [Legends, official] Players under 200 pop or in beginner's protection "can only accept 1:1 offers" and cannot send resources directly. This limits parking resources with friends. — [Automatic resource-sharing limits](https://support.travian.com/en/articles/125-automatic-resource-sharing-limits)
- [Legends] Gaul trappers capture attackers in raids and attacks. Only an attack tries to free trapped troops, and "25% of troops released that way will die". — [Unofficial Travian: Battle Mechanics](https://unofficialtravian.com/2025/10/battle-mechanics/)
- [Commentary, Excerpt-only] TV Tropes describes an "Early Game Hell": new players are exposed once protection ends, and a strong alliance is the main way to stay safe. It says updates tried to ease this with a task system that pays rewards into the hero inventory, which cannot be stolen until transferred. — [TV Tropes: Travian](https://tvtropes.org/pmwiki/pmwiki.php/VideoGame/Travian)
- [T3, Anecdotes, 2008 comments] Without early play a newcomer is "merely placed at the mercy of those players that have already built up". Once a server is over two weeks old, a casual player without gold has "no way... to have any fun". — [JayIsGames review comments](https://jayisgames.com/review/travian.php)
- [T3, Anecdote, 2009–10, Excerpt-only] Veterans boast about raiding newcomers. One poster calls it a design flaw, and veterans advising new players to restart drives them away. — [MMORPG.com forum thread](https://forums.mmorpg.com/discussion/257091/just-because-you-dont-know-how-to-play)
- [Anecdote, date unknown, Excerpt-only] "my beginner protection just ran out and im getting heavily farmed". — [Overclockers UK forum, Travian UK server thread](https://www.overclockers.co.uk/forums/threads/travian-uk-server-2.17766555/page-3)
- [Kingdoms, special worlds only] **Loot limit**:
  - A daily cap on resources a player can take by raiding or sieging other *player* villages. Nature, Robbers and Natars are exempt.
  - Stated purpose: to balance raiding and encourage more strategic play.
  - When a raid would exceed the cap, the excess stays with the defender. The resource value of the attacker's own troops lost in that battle doesn't count toward the cap.
  - Cap = base (rising weekly with server age and speed) + attacker points × speed. At 3x the base is 30,000 in week 1, 75,000 in week 2, 150,000 in week 3, and 450,000 from week 7.
  - Halloween Hunt 2026 (5x): base +37,500/day, reaching 900,000 by day 25.
  - A warning appears at 80%.
  — [Kingdoms support: Loot limit](https://support.kingdoms.com/en/articles/187-loot-limit)
- [Kingdoms, Excerpt-only] Kingdoms protection is 7 days at x1, 4 at x3 and 3 at x5. It ends automatically at 200 population or when the second village is settled. — [Kingdoms support: Beginner's protection](https://support.kingdoms.com/en/articles/4-beginner-s-protection)

### Inferences
- At x1 a level-10 cranny shelters 8,000 resources (2,000 × 4; Gauls 12,000). That is about a day's output for a ~300/hour early village, so a newcomer who builds the cranny and keeps spending is a poor farm. This fits guide advice to farm inactives, who don't spend.
- The real risk for small players is overflow: idle warehouses, offline nights and market offers. The players who lose most are therefore the ones who log in least, i.e. the casual players the 2008–2010 complaints describe.
- Kingdoms' loot cap is low early and rises with server age and the raider's attacker points. That signals the designers see uncapped player-vs-player looting in a server's first weeks as the most distorting. It is a Kingdoms special-world experiment, not a Legends rule.

### Gaps
- I found no official Travian Games data or statements (Legends, 2019–2026) linking raiding to new-player churn.
- No measured figures for how much a typical undefended small Legends player loses per day or week.
- Official forum design threads could not be reached (hosts did not resolve), and Reddit threads were inaccessible.

## 5. Costs and risks to the raider

### Takeaway
Raiding costs:
- training and crop upkeep;
- losses to defenders, traps and oasis animals;
- hero health and revives;
- sustained attention (hourly sends, farm-list curation, sitters);
- money (farm lists require the Gold Club; the wave builder is 50 Gold per village).

Official rules of thumb set the break-even. An army must pay for its training and upkeep, and oasis losses should be at most half the bounty. A hard cap of 20,000 outgoing troops limits throughput. Raiding a linked account is blocked by pushing protection.

### Cited Findings
- [Legends, official] The Farm List is "a premium feature included in the Gold Club". It can send raids to all targets at once with a single click. Lists belong to a village, and an entry containing only spies sends a scouting mission. — [Farm Lists (Travian support)](https://support.travian.com/en/articles/69-farm-lists)
- [Legends] The wave builder costs 50 Gold per village and sends up to eight waves with one click. — [Unofficial Travian: 6 years of changes](https://unofficialtravian.com/2025/01/travian-legends-6-years-of-changes-at-a-glance/)
- [Legends] The outgoing-troop limit is 20,000, including raids. Once it is reached, farm-list sends fail with an error until troops return. — [Unofficial Travian: Battle Mechanics](https://unofficialtravian.com/2025/10/battle-mechanics/)
- [Legends] Hospital: healing wounded troops costs the same as training but takes half the time. Bandages on the hero reduce battle losses. — [Unofficial Travian: Battle Mechanics](https://unofficialtravian.com/2025/10/battle-mechanics/)
- [Legends, official] For oasis hunting, troop losses should be at most half the bounty. Keep ointments, and ideally a water bucket, to heal or revive the hero. — [Oasis Farming (After Beginner Protection)](https://support.travian.com/en/articles/185-oasis-farming-after-beginner-protection)
- [Legends, official blog, x2] "The army should either give additional income that covers costs to their training and upkeep". — [ROI article (repost)](https://unofficialtravian.com/2025/01/early-development-return-on-investment/)
- [Legends, NL special] The Viking Berserker eats twice as much as other infantry, an example of upkeep as a tuning lever. — [Unofficial Travian: Northern Legends mechanics](https://unofficialtravian.com/2025/10/northern-legends-game-mechanics/)
- [Legends, official] Pushing protection:
  - Raiding unrelated players is unrestricted.
  - Limits apply when attacker and defender are in the same confederacy or share a recent IP or sitter link (Type 2/3). The defender is then treated as if sending resources to the attacker.
  - If a Type-3 connection recently sent resources to the defender, the attacker cannot steal any.
  — [Automatic resource-sharing limits](https://support.travian.com/en/articles/125-automatic-resource-sharing-limits)
- [T4, Anecdote] Attention costs:
  - Send raids "once an hour".
  - Farm-list setup took "around 3 hours work".
  - With only 2-hour logins, "it may be worth adding an extra troop per farm".
  - A level-1 hero took "2 hours to revive, and cost 1050 resources".
  — [T4 Start Game Guide](https://lordsoftravian.forumotion.com/t5-t4-start-game-guide)
- [T4] A Teuton guide recommends two sitters so raids run 24/7. — [T4 Teuton Full Guide](http://travian4guides.blogspot.com/p/t4-teuton-full-guide.html)
- [T3, Excerpt-only] A top-10 raider needs 50+ raids in flight at all times for 48 hours. — [Travian Library: Growth Guide](https://travianlibrary.wordpress.com/2009/03/13/growth-guide/)
- [Kingdoms, Excerpt-only] Regular losses can quickly eat the gains, and over-farming an active player "may awaken a sleeping giant" (retaliation). — [Kingdoms: The Art of Micro-Farming](https://support.kingdoms.com/en/articles/155-the-art-of-micro-farming)
- [Third-party, Excerpt-only] Browser extensions sell farm-list automation: an "Automated Farm List" that builds inactive-farm lists and raids them automatically, and raid-interval timers. This points to farm-list upkeep as a burden players will pay or script to avoid. — [Travian Helper listing (extscope)](https://extscope.org/extension/gjldcdgklahofngbhilkhciibpcfngll)
- [Legends, Oct 17 2024, Excerpt-only] A quality-of-life update added an option to generate raid reports only when losses occur, a scout icon for spy raids sent from farm lists, and removal of a village from all farm lists at once. — [Travian: Legends October 2024 QoL update](https://www.travian.com/international/news/2024/10/17/travian-legends-october-2024-quality-of-life-updat/)

### Inferences
- The fixed costs are the Gold Club, the hours of farm-list setup, hourly sends and sitters. They make raid returns concentrate among high-attention and often paying players. A casual player cannot reach Top-10 volumes however efficient their troops are.
- Loss risk is managed by scouting, target selection (inactives only) and simulation. The official "losses ≤ ½ bounty" rule implies typical PvE raids are designed to clear at least a 2:1 return.

### Gaps
- Gold Club price and duration for Legends not found.
- Per-unit carry capacity and upkeep tables could not be retrieved (Kirilloid is script-rendered; Fandom blocked). The only figure found is from a low-quality blog: Legionnaire carries 50, and 100 Legionnaires cost 40,000.
- No data on typical raid loss rates or retaliation frequency.

## 6. Design intent and tuning history (about 2017–2026)

### Takeaway
The documented Legends changes mostly made raiding **more convenient and better instrumented**: the farm-list overhaul with income stats, Start all, scout lists, report filtering and bulk removal. They also gave raiding a sanctioned **PvE outlet**: the oasis animal bounty, called "one of the most impactful changes", and a separate PvE weekly ranking. Abuse was closed off: pushing protection counts raid income and blocks raids between linked accounts. Rounds were shortened and early rewards made fairer. I found **no Legends cap on player-vs-player loot**. The explicit loot limit exists only on selected Kingdoms special worlds (2025–2026), with the stated aim "to balance raiding and encourage more strategic play".

### Cited Findings
- [Legends] Change list (community, originally May 2023, republished 2025):
  - Huns and Egyptians added in 2017.
  - Spartans added in 2022, with an Asclepeion in place of the Hospital.
  - Vikings added in 2024.
  - Shorter rounds (2021): x1 rounds rarely exceed 200 days and cannot exceed 250, down from 350. The stated aim is that this "made the game more dynamic".
  - Early-game randomness removed: fixed first-ten adventures, daily quests and task rewards, and a horse in the first adventure, "to make the early game fairer".
  - Oasis farming and nature-kill rewards: described as one of the most important changes of the preceding two years, which "has affected the gameplay a lot".
  - Weekly attacker ranking split into PvE and PvP.
  - Farm lists overhauled: average farm income and total farm per day shown; copy a target into several lists; targets that change owner auto-deactivate; lists from destroyed villages can be reactivated; scout farm lists; a "Start all" button.
  - Wave builder (50 Gold per village), Hospital, travel-time simulator.
  - Community Week tribe rebalancing (2023, special worlds only).
  — [Unofficial Travian: 6 years of changes](https://unofficialtravian.com/2025/01/travian-legends-6-years-of-changes-at-a-glance/)
- [Legends, Excerpt-only] An official forum announcement titled "🔥 COMING SOON 🔥 Farm List Improvements" existed. The host no longer resolves, so its date and content are unverified. — [Travian: Legends Forum thread 646576](https://wbb.forum.travian.com/index.php?thread%2F646576-coming-soon-farm-list-improvements%2F=)
- [Legends, official] The oasis animal bounty is called "one of the most impactful changes in Travian: Legends". No stated purpose beyond "a strong economic advantage" for those who learn it early. — [Early Game Oasis Farming](https://support.travian.com/en/articles/190-early-game-oasis-farming)
- [Legends, official blog, Apr 2023] The designers' blog teaches an ROI rule for armies: they must cover training and upkeep. — [ROI article (repost)](https://unofficialtravian.com/2025/01/early-development-return-on-investment/)
- [Legends, Oct 17 2024, Excerpt-only] Quality-of-life update: raid reports only when losses occur, a scout icon for farm-list spy raids, and removal of a village from all farm lists. — [travian.com news](https://www.travian.com/international/news/2024/10/17/travian-legends-october-2024-quality-of-life-updat/)
- [Legends, 2024 Annual Special / spring 2025, Excerpt-only] Vikings and raid ships:
  - Vikings arrived with Northern Legends. Raid ships are the only ships allowed in farm lists.
  - Attacks and raids show in the alliance member list.
  - The Viking hero can lower loyalty by 5%.
  — [Unofficial Travian: Northern Legends mechanics](https://unofficialtravian.com/2025/10/northern-legends-game-mechanics/); [travian.com: Travian: Northern Legends](https://www.travian.com/international/news/2025/03/20/travian-northern-legends-spring-round/). A special 2025 spring round made the Vikings temporarily playable. — [Wikipedia: Travian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Travian)
- [Legends, Mar 31 2026, Excerpt-only] Changelog 417.3 re-added missing oasis coordinates to farm lists. — [travian.com: Changelog 417.3](https://www.travian.com/us/news/2026/03/31/changelog-417-3/)
- [Legends, official, undated] Pushing protection counts "average hourly raid income over the past 7 days" toward the sender's production. Raids between confederates or linked accounts are treated as resource transfers and limited. — [Automatic resource-sharing limits](https://support.travian.com/en/articles/125-automatic-resource-sharing-limits)
- [T4.4, Excerpt-only] Beginner's protection became non-cancellable and lasts 5 days (x1) from Travian 4.4. — [Travian Wiki (Fandom): Teutons](https://travian.fandom.com/wiki/Teutons)
- [Kingdoms, 2025–2026, special worlds] Two more levers on selected special worlds:
  - The loot limit (daily cap on loot from player villages, scaled by server age, speed and attacker points; stated purpose "balance raiding and encourage more strategic play"). — [Kingdoms: Loot limit](https://support.kingdoms.com/en/articles/187-loot-limit)
  - Treasure restrictions (whether treasures can be stolen depends on the defender's Victory Points relative to the attacker's). — [Kingdoms: Treasure restrictions](https://support.kingdoms.com/en/articles/186-treasure-restrictions)
- [Kingdoms, May 2025, Excerpt-only] Robber Camps reworked (wilderness placement, no camp attacks, rally system, diminishing returns when overcrowded). — [Kingdoms blog](https://blog.kingdoms.com/theyve-found-us-a-robbers-take-on-being-reworked/)
- [T3-era design commentary, 2007] Edery argued that fixed per-village build times preserve real-time feel while "effectively preventing people with tons of spare time from overwhelming competing players" (Excerpt-only). Raiding is the system that does convert spare time into growth. — [Edery: Travian Under the Microscope](https://edery.org/2007/03/travian-under-the-microscope/)

### Inferences
- Legends has kept raiding's ceiling intact. The documented tuning is about convenience (farm lists), visibility (income stats, PvE/PvP split), integrity (anti-push) and a PvE alternative that doesn't feed on other players (the oasis bounty). The new-player brakes are structural (protection length, fairer early rewards, unstealable hero-inventory rewards, shorter rounds), not a loot cap.
- The Kingdoms loot limit (special worlds) is the closest thing to an explicit company position that unrestricted player raiding needs balancing. Its design (low early, rising with server age; the raider's own losses excluded) targets exactly the early-server window where raid multiples are highest (Section 2).

### Gaps
- No dates found for the Legends farm-list overhaul, the Hospital, or the PvE/PvP ranking split. The official forum and news pages could not be read in full.
- No official Travian Games statement (Legends, 2019–2026) found on the intended balance between raiding and building, or on raiding and new-player retention.
- I could not verify the Travian: Legends launch year or the version history of the cranny and Teuton modifiers. Sources conflict on the Gaul cranny (1.5× vs 2×) and on whether the Teuton cranny reduction is tribe-wide or hero-only.
